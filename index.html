ABOUT

A little about me, beyond the pixels.

I'm naturally curiuos and tend to ask a lot of "why" questions. I love digging into how something works, understanding where people are getting stuck, and finding the simpler path forward. My favorite projects are the ones where design can imporve not only the interface, but the way people actually work.

Outside of design, life is a little less structured. You'll usually find me spending time with my husband and son, planning our next trip, trying something new in the kitchen, or finding an excuse to get outside.

I bring that same curiosity into my work – thoughtful about the details, open to new ideas, and always looking for a better way to solve the problem.
