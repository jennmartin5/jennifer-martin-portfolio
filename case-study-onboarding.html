CASE STUDY

Role-Based Product Onboarding

A centralized learning experience designed to help clinical users find the right training and resources faster.

UX STRATEGY USER FLOW UI DESIGN

THE CHALLENGE

Make training easier to find and easier to use.

Clinical users needed a clearer way to access onboarding materials, training videos, guides, and resources without relying on a separate training destination.

MY ROLE

From concept to structured onboarding experience.

I developed the onboarding concept, mapped the user and admin experiences, organized content around user roles, and designed the interface and supporting flows.

THE APPROACH

Organize content around what each user actually needs.

The experience combines role-based onboarding with guides, videos, searchable resources, and a getting-started checklist so users can quickly identify the materials most relevant to their responsibilities.

OUTCOME

A scalable foundation for product education.

The concept provides a structured foundation for bringing training directly into the product experience while reducing dependence on separate training resources.
